01INTRODUCTION

Welcome to ConnectX

ConnectX (“ConnectX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a dating and social-discovery application that allows users to create profiles, discover other users, communicate, match with other users, and use related social and dating features.

This Privacy Policy explains how ConnectX collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and otherwise processes personal information when you use our application, website, services, and related features.

By using ConnectX, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

02INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the features you use

A. Account Information

  • Name or display name
  • Date of birth
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Login credentials or authentication information
  • Profile photograph
  • Account ID

B. Profile Information

You may voluntarily provide:

  • Profile photographs
  • Bio/about-me information
  • Hobbies
  • Interests
  • Education information
  • Occupation
  • Relationship preferences
  • Dating preferences
  • Languages
  • Lifestyle preferences
  • Other information you choose to add to your profile
Information placed on a public or discoverable profile may be visible to other ConnectX users.

C. Verification Information

Where ConnectX provides verification features, we may collect information required to verify age, identity, profile authenticity, photograph authenticity, and gender, where applicable to the verification feature.

If verification requires documents, photographs, video, audio, facial comparison, or other information, we will use such information for the stated verification, safety, security, fraud-prevention, and legal-compliance purposes.

ConnectX will not claim that verification guarantees that another user is safe, genuine, or trustworthy.

D. Location Information

With appropriate permission, ConnectX may collect approximate location, device location, city or region, and location-related information used for matching and discovery. ConnectX may use approximate location rather than displaying your exact location to other users. You may control location permissions through your device settings.

E. Communication Information

If you use ConnectX messaging or communication features, we may process information relating to those communications for purposes such as providing messaging functionality, preventing spam, detecting fraud, investigating abuse, protecting users, enforcing our Terms, and complying with applicable law.

F. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect IP address, device type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, browser information, language settings, network information, crash reports, log information, and usage information.

G. Usage Information

We may collect information about how you use ConnectX, including features used, profiles viewed, matches, likes and interactions, search preferences, app activity, login and logout information, technical events, and performance information.

03HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

ConnectX may use personal information to

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide dating and social-discovery services.
  • Create and display your profile.
  • Provide matching and recommendation features.
  • Enable communication between users.
  • Provide age and identity verification.
  • Detect fake accounts and fraudulent activity.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, harassment, and scams.
  • Protect users and platform security.
  • Process payments and subscriptions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Respond to complaints and reports.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Develop and improve ConnectX features.
  • Conduct analytics and operational measurement.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Send promotional communications where legally permitted and according to your preferences.
  • Comply with legal obligations and lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, property, and safety of ConnectX, its users, and others.
  • Investigate suspected violations of our Terms and policies.

04LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Where applicable, ConnectX will process personal information based on appropriate legal grounds, including:

  • Your consent.
  • Provision of services requested by you.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate operational, security, fraud-prevention, or safety purposes where permitted by applicable law.
  • Other lawful grounds available under applicable law.

Where consent is required, we will provide an appropriate consent mechanism.

05CONSENT

Where ConnectX relies on consent, you may be asked to provide consent before certain personal information is collected or used.

You may withdraw consent where legally permitted. Withdrawal of consent may affect your ability to use certain features or services that require the relevant information. Withdrawal of consent will not necessarily affect processing already lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

06PUBLIC AND DISCOVERABLE PROFILE INFORMATION

Certain information may be visible to other ConnectX users, depending on your profile and privacy settings. This may include:

  • Display name
  • Age
  • Profile photographs
  • Bio
  • Interests
  • Hobbies
  • General location
  • Other profile information you choose to publish
Do not publish information that you do not want other users to see.

07INFORMATION ABOUT OTHER PEOPLE

You must not provide ConnectX with another person's personal information unless you have a lawful basis or appropriate permission to do so.

You must not upload another person's private photographs, documents, contact information, or personal data without authorization.

ConnectX may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, local storage, analytics technologies, or similar technologies on its website or application. These technologies may be used for:

  • Security
  • Remembering preferences
  • Analytics
  • Performance monitoring
  • Fraud prevention
  • Improving user experience
  • Advertising, where applicable

11PAYMENTS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

ConnectX may provide paid subscriptions, premium features, boosts, virtual items, or other paid services. Payment information may be processed by third-party payment processors, app stores, or other payment providers.

ConnectX may share information with the following categories of recipients where appropriate and legally permitted:

Information you voluntarily make visible on your ConnectX profile may be shown to other users.

B. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers for cloud hosting, database services, analytics, customer support, identity verification, fraud detection, security, notifications, payment processing, email/SMS services, and technical infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf for specified purposes.

C. Legal Authorities

We may disclose information where required or permitted by applicable law, court order, lawful governmental request, or other legal process.

D. Safety and Fraud Prevention

We may share appropriate information when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, threats, security incidents, or illegal activity, subject to applicable law.

E. Business Transfers

If ConnectX is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar transaction, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable legal requirements.

ConnectX does not sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product.

If our business model changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Security measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Authentication controls
  • Monitoring
  • Secure infrastructure
  • Logging
  • Security testing
  • Internal policies

However, no Internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You are also responsible for protecting your password, OTP, device, and account credentials.

15DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Retention periods may depend on:

  • Whether your account remains active.
  • The purpose for which information was collected.
  • Legal obligations.
  • Fraud and safety requirements.
  • Dispute resolution.
  • Security requirements.
  • Financial and transaction records.

16ACCOUNT DELETION

You may request deletion of your ConnectX account through the available account settings or by contacting ConnectX Support.

When your account is deleted, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize personal information that we are no longer required or permitted to retain.

Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law, including information necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, financial records, and enforcement of agreements.

ConnectX is strictly intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly permit persons under 18 to create or use ConnectX accounts.
